Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the rclone application ("rclone", "the application", "we", "us") accesses, uses, stores and protects information, including data obtained from Google when you connect rclone to your Google account. rclone is a Google OAuth client, operated by an independent developer (GitHub: kaczmarekb-eng), that connects the open-source rclone command-line program to the Google Drive API. More information about the application is available on the home page.

Summary: rclone runs only on your own computer. Your files, file metadata and Google OAuth tokens stay on your computer and on Google's servers. The developer does not operate any server that receives your data, and never sees, collects, stores, sells or shares your Google user data.

1. Google user data accessed

When you add a Google Drive remote with rclone config, you are redirected to Google's consent screen and asked to grant rclone access to your Google Drive. Depending on the access level you choose during configuration, rclone requests one of the following OAuth scopes:

ScopeWhat it allowsWhy rclone needs it
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive See, edit, create and delete all of your Google Drive files. Default option. Required to back up, sync, download, move and delete any files and folders you choose in your Drive.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly See and download all of your Google Drive files. Used if you configure rclone in read-only mode, e.g. only to download or list files.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file See, edit, create and delete only the files created or opened with rclone. Used if you want rclone to access only files that it uploaded itself.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata Access a hidden application-specific folder in your Drive. Used if you want rclone to store data only in its private application folder.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly View metadata (names, sizes, dates) of your files, but not their contents. Used if you only want to list files and check their sizes and dates.

Through the granted scope, rclone may process the following data, only while executing a command you started:

  • File contents of the files you choose to upload, download, copy or sync;
  • File and folder metadata, such as names, sizes, MIME types, checksums, modification times, folder structure and trash status;
  • OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) issued by Google, which authorize rclone to call the Google Drive API on your behalf.

rclone does not request access to your contacts, e-mail, calendar, location or any other Google service.

2. How Google user data is used

Google user data is used solely to provide the features that you explicitly request, namely:

  • listing files and folders in your Google Drive;
  • uploading (backing up) files from your computer to your Google Drive;
  • downloading (restoring) files from your Google Drive to your computer;
  • synchronizing, copying, moving, renaming and deleting files and folders at your command;
  • comparing sizes, dates and checksums to verify that transfers are complete and correct.

Google user data is not used:

  • for advertising, including personalized or retargeted advertising;
  • to build user profiles, for analytics, market research or credit-worthiness assessment;
  • to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models;
  • for any purpose other than providing and improving the user-facing file-transfer features of rclone.
Limited Use disclosure. rclone's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data storage and retention

  • No developer servers. rclone has no backend. All communication takes place directly between your computer and Google's servers. The developer never receives your files, metadata or tokens.
  • OAuth tokens are saved only on your computer, in the local rclone configuration file (usually ~/.config/rclone/rclone.conf on Linux/macOS or %APPDATA%\rclone\rclone.conf on Windows). They are kept until you delete the remote or the file, or revoke access in your Google account.
  • File contents and metadata are processed in memory only for the duration of the command you run. Files you download are written to the local location you specify; files you upload are stored in your own Google Drive. rclone keeps no other copies.
  • Logs, if you enable them, are written only to your own computer and are under your control.

4. Data sharing and disclosure

rclone does not sell, rent, trade, transfer or disclose Google user data to any third party. No Google user data is shared with the developer, advertisers, data brokers or any other person or service. The only party that receives data is Google itself, as necessary to perform the operations you request through the Google Drive API.

Because the developer does not hold any of your data, the developer cannot disclose it in response to requests from third parties.

5. Data protection and security

  • All communication with Google APIs is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS).
  • Authorization uses Google's standard OAuth 2.0 flow; rclone never sees or stores your Google password.
  • Tokens are stored only locally. rclone supports encrypting the entire configuration file with a password (rclone config → "Set configuration password"), which we recommend.
  • Only the minimum scope you choose during configuration is requested.

6. Your control: revoking access and deleting data

You remain in full control of your data at all times:

  • Revoke access: go to https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, select rclone and choose "Remove access". The stored tokens immediately stop working.
  • Delete local tokens: run rclone config delete <remote-name>, or delete the rclone configuration file.
  • Delete files: your files remain in your Google Drive and on your computer, where you can manage or delete them at any time.

Because no data is stored by the developer, there is nothing to delete on the developer's side. If you still have a question or a data-related request, contact us (see section 10) and we will respond within 30 days.

7. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics, tracking scripts or forms, and it does not require signing in. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process technical data such as IP addresses in server logs for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Children

rclone is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly process data of children.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. As described above, the developer does not store your personal data; any data processed by rclone is under your own control on your computer and in your Google account.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about how rclone handles your data, please contact us via GitHub Issues.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how Google user data is used, we will update this page before the change takes effect, and where required we will ask for your renewed consent.
